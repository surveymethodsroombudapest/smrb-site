**The ELTE Survey Methods Room Budapest is a research group within the Department of Statistics at ELTE’s Faculty of Social Sciences that focuses on survey methodology. Our work centers on the question of how to collect reliable data in a world where social research methods and the data collection environment are constantly changing.**

**About survey-based data collection**

Surveys are one of the most important data sources in the social sciences. They help us learn about people's opinions, attitudes, values, living conditions and behavior, making them indispensable in understanding social processes. The results of survey research are used, among others, in public opinion polls, socio-political analyses, market research, and numerous domestic and international scientific studies. The quality of survey data is therefore not merely a methodological issue: it fundamentally determines the extent to which we can draw reliable conclusions about the functioning of society.

**About survey methodology**

Survey methodology examines how each step of data collection – sampling, questionnaire design, data collection methods, response rate, weighting or statistical estimation – influences the results of research. The spread of online surveys, the declining response rate, and the emergence of new data sources pose fundamental challenges to social science research. The purpose of survey methodology is to provide scientifically grounded answers to these challenges and to ensure that the conclusions drawn from research are reliable, valid and comparable.

**About our focus**

The research conducted at the ELTE Survey Methods Room Budapest lies at the intersection of the social sciences and mathematical statistics. In our work, we examine methodological issues related to the design, implementation, and statistical analysis of questionnaire data. Our goal is to develop and evaluate methods that contribute to improving the data quality of questionnaire-based research and the reliability of empirical social science research.
